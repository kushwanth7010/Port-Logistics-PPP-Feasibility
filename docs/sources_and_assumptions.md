# Sources, provenance and limitations

## Synthetic case inputs
Every numerical input in `data/assumptions.json` is **a deliberately invented case assumption**. No port authority, client or cargo operator has supplied data. Neither the analysis nor its NPV/IRR estimates represent a real port investment.

## Public conceptual references (not sources for model inputs)
- Government of India PPP Structuring Toolkit: https://www.pppinindia.gov.in/ppp_structuring_toolkit
- Government of India PPP Guidance: https://www.pppinindia.gov.in/guidance_material_and_reference_doc
- World Bank PPP risk identification: https://ppp.worldbank.org/identifying-risks
- Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways: https://shipmin.gov.in/

## Simplifications
Construction capex is paid at year 0; concession starts in year 1. No inflation, debt, customs fees, working-capital build, maintenance capex, uncertain commissioning date, terminal-value appraisal, regulatory tariff ceilings or tax losses carried forward. This makes the case useful for project-valuation interviews, but insufficient for real investment approval.
